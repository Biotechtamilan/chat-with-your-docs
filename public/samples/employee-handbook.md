# Tidewater Instruments employee handbook

Tidewater Instruments builds water-quality sensors for utilities and research labs. This handbook covers the policies people ask about most. It applies to all full-time employees unless a section says otherwise.

## Working hours and location

The standard week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:30 local time; outside those hours you set your own schedule with your manager.

Tidewater is a hybrid workplace. Office days are Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday are remote by default. Employees who live more than 90 minutes from an office can apply for a fully remote arrangement, reviewed once a year in January.

## Annual leave

Employees accrue 1.75 days of annual leave for each completed month of service, which is 21 days over a full year. Leave is requested in the people portal at least ten working days in advance for absences longer than three days.

### Carry-over

Up to five unused days carry over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March; any that remain after that date are forfeited. Carry-over above five days needs written approval from a director and is granted only when work demands prevented the leave from being taken.

### Public holidays and sick leave

Public holidays are in addition to annual leave. Sick leave is paid at full salary for the first ten working days in a rolling twelve-month period. A doctor's note is required from the fourth consecutive day of absence.

## Parental leave

Every new parent, regardless of gender, receives 16 weeks of leave at full pay, taken within the first year after birth or adoption. The first six weeks are taken as one block; the remaining ten can be split into two blocks.

## Probation

New employees have a probation period of 90 days. During probation the notice period is one week on either side. After probation it is one month.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Claims submitted after 30 days are paid only with approval from the finance lead.

- A receipt is required for any single item above 40 dollars.
- Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 55 dollars per day.
- Flights under five hours are booked in economy. Longer flights can be booked in premium economy.
- Claims above 1,500 dollars need sign-off from a director before the purchase is made.

Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## Equipment

Each employee receives a laptop and one monitor. A home-office allowance of 400 dollars is available once every three years for a chair or desk. Equipment is returned within ten working days of leaving the company.

## Security

Every account uses the company password manager and two-factor authentication. Sharing credentials is never acceptable, including with colleagues.

Report a suspected phishing message to security@tidewater.example and do not click its links. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT desk within one hour of discovery so that the device can be wiped remotely.

Customer sensor data must not be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 dollars per calendar year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.
